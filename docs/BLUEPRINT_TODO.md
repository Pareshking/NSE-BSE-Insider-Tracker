# Blueprint review: tracker (opened 15 Oct 2026)

Every point of the owner's "Master Blueprint" (a different project, shared to fine-tune ours) is listed here by its
section number, nothing dropped. Per point: what we have, the first step, and the owner's call.

## Rules (owner, 15 Oct)

1. **One item at a time, in order** (1.1, 1.1a, 1.2 ... 1.9, then 2.1 ...). An item is closed only when its
   *closes when* line is met and shown (a run, a table, a test). A new item is not opened until the one before is
   closed; a finding that needs more work becomes a new numbered item, never a skipped one.
2. The owner approves, rejects or defers (Approve / Reject / Later) each item before it starts.
3. The blueprint's example companies and figures are not verified and are never used as test truth (rule 7 in
   `CLAUDE.md`); its provider limits and model names are checked against each provider's own page before use.

## Decisions taken while working through it

- **Read every announcement** (owner, 15 Oct), with the cheapest and fastest model that passes our checks and the
  largest limits. This replaces "routine subjects are never read" (reading groups) once item 3.12 shows the
  capacity; until then the reader stays paused.
- **Clean each PDF before it goes to a model** (owner, 15 Oct, *must*): strip logos, letterheads, company and
  exchange addresses, page furniture and other boilerplate, send only what carries information (item 4.14).
- **Collection never depends on reading** (found 15 Oct, item 1.1a): `announcement-collect.yml`.

### 12.23b Accuracy rule decided (17 Oct 2026)

A card goes on only when two independent readings agree and the deterministic checks pass; otherwise it is held with the reason, never corrected. In order: (1) structured source first (XBRL when the filing links one); (2) text read: PyMuPDF `sort=True`, tesseract for pages with no or mangled text, model extraction, every quote must be words of that text; (3) deterministic checks: units, signs ((0.35) is a loss, no % across a loss), results arithmetic (revenue + other income = total income; PBT - tax = PAT), basis label from the heading, multi-item totals; (4) for table cards and cards the checks hold, a second independent reading from the page images (Gemini, page triage, fallback across models): a figure of the text headline the image read does not state is asked of the page, claim by claim, and kept only if the page quotes it and the quote is in the filing's text; otherwise the headline is held ("two readers disagree"); (5) a scan whose pages mostly give no text is marked not read. Not built: PaddleOCR / Docling / Marker (tesseract has been enough; benchmark only if it fails on a measured sample), annual-report statements, a cell-level benchmark (to fold into the audits). Open: a published card should show whether it was agreed by two readers (rule 4).

### 12.25 What is AI and what is code (owner asked, 17 Oct 2026)

AI proposes, code verifies. **AI:** the models that read a filing and write the headline, subtitle, points, fields and metrics, each with a quote (Z.ai GLM, Gemini, Groq Qwen lanes); the Gemini models that read the page images as a second reading and answer the claim-by-claim question "is this figure printed on the page". **Machine-learning, not generative:** OCR (tesseract, PaddleOCR, Docling) turns pixels into text and decides nothing. **Plain code:** PyMuPDF text, page triage, every check (quote found in the filing's text, units, signs, the results arithmetic, Reg 29/31 holdings, shares x price, the agreement of figures between readings, deferral when no page reader answers), the percentage changes, and every number on the site's context lines ("Before this", from our own tables).

What is stored for context: every read in the reads archive (headline, fields, metrics with changes, quotes, reader, prompt version, `second_reader`); per-company memory `archive/company_memory/{ISIN}.json` (last 100 verified events, appended by every live read); the XBRL results history and shareholding tables. Not stored per value: page number, position on the page, OCR engine, the page images or the vision model's raw answer. Retention only prunes dated raw/canonical snapshots the archive already holds.
